Sponsors & Partners

Major commercial and technical partners that support Oracle Red Bull Racing (2026 season focus).

Title & Power Partners

Title Partner

Oracle

Title sponsor since 2022. Multi-year extension announced in 2026. Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and AI tools support strategy, simulations and operations. Team officially races as Oracle Red Bull Racing.

Power Unit

Ford

Power-unit partner for the new 2026 regulations. Red Bull Ford Powertrains develops the DM01 hybrid unit that powers the RB22.

Key Team Partners

Visa

Major partner with branding on the cars and strong presence across both Red Bull Racing and the sister team Racing Bulls. Multi-year extension through 2030.

TAG Heuer

Official timekeeper and timepiece supplier since 2016. Long-standing lifestyle and timing partner.

AT&T

Telecommunications and data partner. Supports the flow of race data between the track and the Milton Keynes factory.

YETI

Official supplier of coolers and drinkware since 2023.

Hard Rock

Hospitality and lifestyle partner with multi-year involvement.

Other Partners

Additional technical and commercial partners include Siemens, Ansys, Mobil 1 / Esso, Sparco, and others that support engineering, lubricants and safety equipment.
