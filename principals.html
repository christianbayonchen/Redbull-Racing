Team Principals

The leaders who have guided Red Bull Racing from its first race to the present day.

2005 – July 2025

Christian Horner

British · Appointed at age 31 – the youngest Team Principal in F1 at the time. Built Red Bull Racing from a midfield team into a multiple championship-winning organisation. Under his leadership the team won 6 Constructors’ and 8 Drivers’ titles and more than 120 Grands Prix. Departed after the 2025 British Grand Prix following 20 years in the role.

July 2025 – Present

Laurent Mekies

French · Previously Team Principal of Racing Bulls (Red Bull’s sister team). Promoted to lead the senior team after Horner’s departure. Oversaw a strong second-half recovery in 2025 and continues in the role for the 2026 season with the new Red Bull Ford power unit.

Key Supporting Figures

Dietrich Mateschitz

Co-founder of Red Bull. Visionary owner who purchased Jaguar Racing and committed the resources to turn it into a championship team. Passed away in 2022.

Helmut Marko

Motorsport advisor and long-time head of the Red Bull junior programme. Instrumental in identifying and promoting talent such as Vettel and Verstappen.

Adrian Newey

Chief Technical Officer for many years. Designer behind the dominant RB5–RB9 and later ground-effect cars. Left for Aston Martin after the 2024 season.
