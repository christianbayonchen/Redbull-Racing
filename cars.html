The Cars

From the early Cosworth and Ferrari-powered machines to the record-breaking RB19 and the new RB22 with Red Bull Ford Powertrains.

2005

RB1

First Red Bull car. Cosworth V10. Drivers: David Coulthard, Christian Klien, Vitantonio Liuzzi. Solid midfield debut.

2009

RB5

Adrian Newey’s first major Red Bull design. First pole and first win (China). 6 victories. Laid the foundation for the title years.

2010–2013

RB6 – RB9

Four consecutive championship cars. Exhaust-blown diffusers, exceptional aero efficiency. Vettel’s four titles.

2016

RB12

Verstappen’s first Red Bull car. Spanish GP win on debut – youngest race winner in F1 history.

2021

RB16B

Honda-powered. Verstappen’s first World Championship after the dramatic Abu Dhabi finale.

2022

RB18

Ground-effect regulations. First Constructors’ title since 2013. Verstappen’s second Drivers’ crown (15 wins).

2023

RB19

Most dominant car of the modern era. 21 wins from 22 races. Record-breaking season for Verstappen and the team.

2024

RB20

Fourth consecutive Drivers’ title for Verstappen. Team finished third in Constructors.

2025

RB21

Honda RBPT power. Strong recovery in the second half of the season under new leadership.

2026

RB22

First car powered by Red Bull Ford Powertrains DM01 (1.6 V6 turbo hybrid). Driven by Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar.
